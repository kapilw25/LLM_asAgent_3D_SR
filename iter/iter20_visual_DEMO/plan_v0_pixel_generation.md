This makes it a much stronger candidate for rendering V-JEPA future representations into realistic future images and eventually future videos. Start with **single-frame prediction at a +1 second horizon**, then progressively extend to **multiple horizons (+0.5 s, +1 s, +2 s, +3 s)**. The immediate goal is to obtain a high-quality proof of concept demonstrating that V-JEPA predictive latents can be rendered into realistic future images.
